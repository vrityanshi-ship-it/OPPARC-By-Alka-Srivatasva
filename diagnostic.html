CAREER MARKET DIAGNOSTIC

Where do you really stand in the market?

Your experience tells one story. The market may be reading another.

The OPPARC Career Market Diagnostic is designed to identify the forces influencing your current professional position — and the constraints affecting your next move.

Begin the Diagnostic ↓

WHY DIAGNOSE?

Before you change your career, understand your market position.

A career move should begin with clarity, not assumption.

The diagnostic examines how your experience, positioning, visibility, perception and access interact within the career market.

The objective is not to produce a meaningless score. It is to identify where friction exists and what may be constraining opportunity.

THE DIAGNOSTIC LENS

Six dimensions.
One market position.

01

Positioning

How clearly is your professional value defined and differentiated?

02

Visibility

How visible are you to the people and markets that matter?

03

Perception

What is the market likely to understand about your professional proposition?

04

Access

What level of access do you currently have to relevant opportunities and networks?

05

Transferability

How effectively can your capabilities travel across roles, functions or markets?

06

Professional Equity

What accumulated value does your experience create in the current career market?
